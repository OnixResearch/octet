The lint reports inline numeric policy thresholds in production comparisons where the peer expression carries a domain/unit-bearing name. It suppresses generated spans, Octet test contexts, conventional literals (`0`, `1`, `-1`), named constants/statics, unit-bearing locals/constants, and constructor/wrapper argument positions.

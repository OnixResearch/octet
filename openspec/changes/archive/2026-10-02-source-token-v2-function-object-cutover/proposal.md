## Why

At the original proposal baseline, Octet's corrected scanner preserved literal contents and code after a quoted `//`, but pinned Valence accepted only `octet-source-token-v1`, the old byte identity. The former caller compiled against that pin, requested `octet-source-token-v2`, and failed closed at construction with Valence's normalization-algorithm mismatch; `object corpus check` reported typed `source-selection` invalid rather than a v1 match. Before this change's OpenSpec archive, the accepted `openspec/specs/standards-codification/spec.md` still required the checked-in positive v1 corpus receipt to replay as `status=match`. Emitting corrected bytes under v1 or silently changing that receipt would falsely satisfy the requirement. H1/H2 approved a selected v2 pin and replacement delta; H3 guidance review and source-backed V1–V17 verification also completed. Promotion must use the supported OpenSpec archive/spec-sync, not direct canonical-spec editing.

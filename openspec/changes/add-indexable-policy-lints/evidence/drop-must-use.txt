## Implementation

Implemented `drop_must_use` as a late safety lint in `src/safety/drop_must_use.rs`.
Registered it in:

- `src/safety/mod.rs`
- `src/lib.rs` lint inventory
- `src/diagnostics.rs` diagnostic metadata and safety group membership
- snapshot fixtures under `tests/fixtures/`
- UI fixture corpus in `tests/fixtures/ui_fixture_set.json`
- emitting fixture coverage in `tests/fixtures/lint_emitting_fixtures.json`

The lint recognizes explicit `std::mem::drop(...)` / imported `drop(...)` calls whose argument is either:

- a must-use type such as `Result`, `Option`, or an ADT with `#[must_use]`; or
- a direct function/method call whose callee has `#[must_use]`.

The lint suppresses diagnostics in test-only contexts via `test_context::late_hir_id_is_in_test_context`.

real stage outputs, `cargo-octet/src/prove.rs` builds the Octet envelope and
artifact bundle, and `cargo-octet/src/artifact.rs` verifies the frozen bundle.

`fixtures/valid/real_fixture`; negative paths are the unverified Nickel
fixture plus the existing artifact tamper/fail-closed tests.

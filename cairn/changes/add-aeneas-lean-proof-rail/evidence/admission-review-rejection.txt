## Deterministic blockers

- `cargo-octet/src/prove.rs` measured the selected theorem source and copied that
  same digest into `approved_statement_blake3`. No independently configured
  expected digest existed in the proof-rail contract or manifest, so any loaded
  theorem could self-approve its statement identity.

  `NonVacuous`, contrary to the accepted requirement to reject `True`-style
  vacuous statements before property promotion.
- The prior focused tests covered the exact `True` fixture but did not include a
  configured-hash mismatch or parenthesized/whitespace `True` negative.

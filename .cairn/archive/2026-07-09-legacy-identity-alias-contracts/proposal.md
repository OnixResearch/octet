## Why

Several stack surfaces retain legacy names such as Slotcar, Tigerstyle, Basalt's former crate name, and Molten/Aspen labels. Those aliases are sometimes compatibility surfaces and sometimes stale identity drift. Octet should provide a contract pattern for allowed legacy aliases and retirement plans.

## What Changes

- Add namespace compatibility contracts for legacy identity aliases.
- Record canonical name, legacy alias, allowed paths, retirement status, tests, and non-claims.
- Add negative fixtures for unapproved alias use and missing retirement rationale.
